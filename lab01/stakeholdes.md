# Stakeholders: first list

The full list is in [stakeholders.csv](stakeholders.csv) (11 rows). Lab-11 will refine it into a stakeholder register and an engagement plan.

## Priority stakeholders
Besides the project manager herself (S02), two stakeholders combine high influence with high interest: the sponsor, the Head of the IT department (S01), and the data protection officer (S09). The sponsor funds the project, signs the charter and decides at the week-1, week-7 and week-13 gates, so the PM keeps the sponsor closely informed with a weekly status report and a decision-ready summary before each gate. The data protection officer must approve how student names and results are stored before the pilot, so the PM contacts this person early, in the first planning weeks, with a one-page data description rather than at the end when changes are expensive.

## Veto stakeholder
The data protection officer (S09) could stop the project, because the week-14 pilot with 50 students cannot run without approval for the storage of student data.
